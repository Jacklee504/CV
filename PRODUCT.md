# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters and hiring managers reviewing a software engineering candidate, often scanning quickly on desktop or mobile.

## Product Purpose

Present verified experience, projects, skills, education, credentials, and contact paths clearly enough for a recruiter to assess fit and open the CV.

## Brand Personality

Sleek, measured, and technically capable. The presentation should feel polished and personal while keeping professional evidence easy to read.

## Anti-references

Explosive effects, cluttered layouts, novelty that competes with credentials, and motion that delays reading. The existing editorial site direction is approved and should be preserved.

## Design Principles

- Make evidence scannable before adding flourish.
- Use detail and interaction to show craft.
- Keep motion optional, purposeful, and easy to remove from the local experiment.
- Preserve the site's established visual language and content accuracy.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keep content readable and operable without animation. Respect reduced-motion preferences and maintain keyboard access.
